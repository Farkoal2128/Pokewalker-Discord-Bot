# Review Change

Review in this order:
1. requirement/scope correctness;
2. contract and compatibility impact;
3. test quality and missing cases;
4. security/privacy/failure handling;
5. maintainability and unnecessary complexity;
6. evidence accuracy.

Report concrete findings with severity and affected files/behavior. Avoid style-only churn unless it impairs clarity or safety.
