**Goal.**

**`pokewalker/bot/trade.py`:** pure renderers `render_trade`, `render_picker` and `render_result` (detached state from `trading.view`, no I/O).

- **Trade screen:**
  - "Trade with <@partner>"; "Waiting for them to join: they run /trade member:@you" until they join.
  - "You offer / They offer": name · kind · CP · appraisal stars; a "Special trade: shiny / Legendary / Mythical / starter" line (OD6); "nothing yet" when empty.
  - Confirmation status; "Expires <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC>"; the partner's offered sprite.
  - Buttons: Choose Pokémon (`trade_pick`), Confirm (`trade_confirm`, disabled unless both joined and offered and I have not confirmed this revision), Refresh (`trade_refresh`), Cancel trade (`trade_cancel`).
- **Picker:** "N tradable of M owned · favorites, your companion and gym defenders can't be traded", a Select (`trade_offer`) of up to 10 `#n Name · CP x`, and Prev/Next/Back (`trade_pick_prev`, `trade_pick_next`, `trade_refresh`).
- **Result screen:**
  - Completed: "You received <name> (CP x). It counts for your Pokédex. Open /walker → Storage to see it."
  - Ended: "This trade ended. Use /trade member to start again."
  - No Home or `/walker` button anywhere.

`handlers(walker)` maps the seven `trade_*` actions. Each one requires `selection.trade_id`, saves `{trade_id, trade_revision, phase, offset}` and calls T3–T5. Confirm passes `selection.trade_revision` and `walker.bot.content`.

Notices map from statuses:
- `changed`: "The offer changed — review it and confirm again."
- `ineligible`/`offer_invalid`: "That Pokémon can't be traded: <reason>."
- `capped`: "You can trade again in Xh Ym." (round up to the minute).
- `partner_capped`: "They can't trade again yet."
- `waiting`: "Confirmed. Waiting for them."

**`pokewalker/bot/walker.py`** (at most 6 lines): register `trade.handlers(self)` in the :55-64 block. In `route`, refusals for `trade_*` actions use `TRADE_REOPEN = "Please reopen /trade; this screen is stale or expired."`.
