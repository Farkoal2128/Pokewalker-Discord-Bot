**Goal.**

**`pokewalker/bot/trade.py`:** pure renderers `render_trade`, `render_picker` and `render_result` (detached state in, `dict(embed, view)` out, no I/O).

- **Trade screen:**
  - "Trade with <@partner>".
  - "You offer: …" / "They offer: …", each as name · kind · CP · appraisal stars, plus ✨ Shiny or a Legendary/Mythical/starter "special trade" line (Q5); "nothing yet" when empty.
  - Confirmation status; "Expires <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC>"; the partner's offered sprite as image.
  - Buttons: Choose Pokémon (`trade_pick`), Confirm (`trade_confirm`, disabled unless both offered and I have not confirmed this revision), Refresh (`trade_refresh`), Cancel trade (`trade_cancel`).
- **Picker:**
  - Header "N tradable of M owned · favorites, your companion and gym defenders can't be traded".
  - A Select (`trade_offer`) of up to 10, labelled `#n Name · CP x`, with the UID as value.
  - Buttons: Prev (`trade_pick_prev`), Next (`trade_pick_next`), Back (`trade_refresh`).
- **Result screen:**
  - Completed: "You received <name> (CP x). Open /walker → Storage to see it."
  - Ended: "This trade ended. Use /trade member to start again."
  - No buttons. No screen has a Home or `/walker` button (OD1).

`handlers(walker)` maps the seven `trade_*` actions. Each one:
- requires `selection.trade_id`;
- passes `selection.trade_revision` as the seen revision;
- saves `{trade_id, trade_revision, phase, offset}`;
- maps statuses to notices, e.g. `changed`: "The offer changed — review it and confirm again." and `ineligible`/`offer_invalid`: "That Pokémon can't be traded: <reason>."

The `trade_offer` value must be on the page last shown.

**`pokewalker/bot/walker.py`** (at most 6 lines):
- Register `trade.handlers(self)` in the :55-64 block.
- In `route`, refusals for `trade_*` actions use `TRADE_REOPEN = "Please reopen /trade; this screen is stale or expired."` instead of `REOPEN`.
