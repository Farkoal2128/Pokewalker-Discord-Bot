**Goal.**

- services/research.py `settle_overflow(conn, now, content)` (session-free; requires the writer
  transaction like `send`): rows = `profiles JOIN pokemon ON pokemon_uid=overflow_uid AND
  pokemon.profile_id=profiles.profile_id WHERE pokemon.state='owned' AND caught_at <= now -
  900`. For each, `send(conn, {"guild_id", "user_id", "profile_id", "selection": json.dumps(
  {"single": True, "picks": p, "preview": v})}, p, v, now, content)` with `p = [[uid,
  revision]]`, `v = {"id": f"overflow:{uid}", "expires_at": now + 1, "unlocked": True}`
  (receipt `research:overflow:<uid>`). `sent` → in the same transaction acknowledge its
  `source_encounter_id` (`AND profile_id=? AND acknowledged_at IS NULL`) and clear
  `overflow_uid` (`AND overflow_uid=?`). Other status (excluded, changed) → stays pending; the
  next tick retries. Returns the count sent. `send` itself is unchanged.
- client.py `_sweep_once`: `await research.settle_overflow(conn, now, self.content)` after
  `raids.expire`, same job and `now` (shortcut: a profiles scan per tick, no
  index).
- Cadence: each 60-second tick; restart catch-up is the first tick on ready. A pending choice
  is sent by the first tick at or after `caught_at + 900` (≤ one tick plus writer queue late).
  Until then Send/Upgrade still resolve it; whichever commits first wins (one writer).
- C3: :266-267 receipt text (PLAN Q19 (5)); research paragraph for `settle_overflow`; :934-936
  "then `research.settle_overflow`". C5 :794-795 tick sentence.
