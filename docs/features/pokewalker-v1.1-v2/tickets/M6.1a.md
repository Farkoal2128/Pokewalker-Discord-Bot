  `items`: sorted kinds `{id, name_en}`. `type_chart` over the 18 ordinary types: 200→1.6,
  100→1.0, 50→0.625, 0→0.390625. `PENDING` when a retained non-root species has no incoming
  edge (except 292, 489/490), an item has no English name, or a source has > 25 recipes.
