# M0.8 — D47 accounting: stockout behaviour, unmet ball demand, gym-income comparison

**Tier:** seam (models progression and item rules; its numbers drive G-2)

**Read:** README.md:186-190 (D47: shortfall intended, gym income the extra source), :53 (D23),

**Goal.** Extend `scripts/economy/model.py` and `scripts/simulate_economy.py` so the report
measures ball **demand**, not only stock-limited spending.

- **Stockout behaviour.** The throw policy prefers Poké → Great → Ultra; when the preferred
  type is out it falls back to any ball in stock. With **no balls at all** the model spends
  nothing: the throw is recorded as a **virtual throw** of the ball the policy wanted, and the
  catch roll is resolved exactly as for a real throw, so the encounter leaves the queue when
  it would have with supply and later encounters are not suppressed. **Only the missing ball
  is exempt from consumption:** a berry the policy selects is consumed from stock exactly as
  on a real throw, and once berries run out later attempts use no berry (and the lower
  probability). Ball inventory never changes on a virtual throw and is never negative. The
  D47 Legendary case is reported as part of demand, not hidden by a stuck slot.
- **Counters** per scenario and play style: `balls_supplied` (starter kit, finds,
  `/checkin`, milestones), `balls_spent` (actual, by type), `unmet_ball_demand` (virtual
  throws by type — **labelled estimate: throws the policy would have made with unlimited
  supply**), `stockout_events` (encounters begun with zero balls), `ball_demand = spent +
  unmet`, `deficit = max(0, ball_demand − balls_supplied)`.
- **Gym-income comparison** (moved here from M0.6/M0.7): expected items and balls per
  deployment from `gym_income_table.json` (weights × quantities, one item per full hour held,
  8-hour tenure D24) under two labelled holding assumptions — (a) one 8-hour deployment per
  day, (b) a gym held all day with immediate re-deployment.
- **Report.** A "D47 comparison" table per scenario and style: supplied, spent, unmet demand
  (estimate), deficit, gym balls (a) / (b), and whether (a) closes the deficit. The text
  confirms the shortfall and never tunes it away (D47).
