# M0.6 — Economy model

**Tier:** seam (models step, encounter, item and progression rules)

**Read:** README.md:50-56 (D20–D26), :61 (D29), :186-190 (D47 intended shortfall), :246-256 (D59
sizes), :404-411 (Legendary pacing report), :417-420; research synthesis.md:152-156;
adaptation.md:53-58 (fallback chatter profiles); items-and-catching.md:99 (ball estimate
method); shinies-and-lures.md:53-58; PLAN §4.1 P3–P5.

**Goal.** `scripts/economy/model.py`: a Monte Carlo of one player in one server with an
injected `random.Random`, driven only by `content/balance/*.json` (M0.3), the artifact count
report (M0.4) and `pokewalker.domain.steps` (M0.5) for the D20 curve. Per simulated hour of
activity it draws messages from a chat profile (measured percentiles when available, else the
adaptation.md:53-58 profiles), credits steps, mints encounters at 100 (queue ≤ 3) under two
labelled play styles — **continuous** (the player resolves an encounter as soon as a slot is
needed; used for the D47 top-pace check) and **hourly check** (queue cleared once per active
hour) — rolls tier → identity by route weight (D50 redistribution),
throws with a fixed policy (Poké Ball until 5 left, then Great/Ultra; Razz on Rare+; Master
Ball only on Legendary), applies flee, awards Candy/Stardust (D40a, Pinap not modelled), passive
finds (D21 freeze, overflow to 10 Stardust, rare duplicate 500), `/checkin` once per 60 min of
activity, companion Candy per 150, D59 milestones as they complete, and power-up spending on one
favourite with P3 prices. Ball accounting here is stock-limited (inventory never negative);
stockout behaviour, unmet-demand accounting and the gym-income comparison are M0.8.

Outputs a dict per scenario (1, 4, 8 active hours/day × 30 days): encounters/active hour,
balls supplied vs spent (stock-limited), Candy/Stardust in vs power-up cost to level 30 and 40, days to first shiny
(mean/median), storage fill day at 250, bundle value vs income, expected encounters per
Legendary identity and per named group (README.md:404-411), per-route tier pool sizes.

**Files.** `scripts/economy/__init__.py`, `scripts/economy/model.py` (1 production file,
≤ 300 lines), `tests/test_economy_model.py` (uses `tests/fixtures/content_mini/`).

**Accept.** Tests pass: `test_seeded_run_is_deterministic`, `test_no_negative_balances`,
`test_queue_never_exceeds_three`, `test_find_freeze_three_per_hour`,
`test_checkin_every_60_minutes_of_activity`, `test_legendary_never_flees`,
`test_milestone_awards_once`, `test_uses_measured_profile_when_present`,
`test_fallback_profile_when_absent`,
`test_continuous_style_exceeds_three_encounters_per_hour_with_enough_steps`,
`test_hourly_style_caps_at_three_per_hour`. The model imports and runs offline (no network)
with only the locked dependencies installed; it reuses `pokewalker.domain.steps` (and so
`regex`) rather than duplicating the curve.

**Verify.** `Verification: phase=implementation; scope=focused; coverage=tests above +
git diff --check; receipt=Tests-Focused on staged tree; boundary=SI-0 (G-2)`.
Command: `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p "test_economy_model.py"`.

**Stop if.** The model needs a rule the balance tables do not state (add it to the table in
M0.3's format with its D-number, not as a constant in the script); runtime for 3 scenarios ×
30 days exceeds 60 s on the dev machine (reduce resolution, note it).
